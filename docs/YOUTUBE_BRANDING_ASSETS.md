# XAUUSD Gold Intelligence — YouTube Branding Assets

## Brand direction

**Channel:** XAUUSD Gold Intelligence  
**Positioning:** Gold intelligence for serious XAU/USD traders.  
**Core visual language:** premium dark financial terminal + metallic gold + clean market-chart elements.

## Palette
- Gold: `#D4AF37`
- Dark navy: `#0B1E3A`
- Black: `#000000`
- Green: `#00C853`
- Red: `#FF3830`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Gray: `#8E8E93`
- Deep gold: `#B8860B`

## Typography
- Primary headline: Montserrat Bold / ExtraBold
- Supporting text: Poppins Medium / SemiBold

## Logo
Round black/gold identity with a gold bull, market candles, and XAUUSD as the dominant readable element. The small tagline is secondary and may be omitted at very small sizes.

## Banner
Dark panoramic market environment with XAUUSD / GOLD INTELLIGENCE, gold bull, candlesticks and global-market visual language. Keep critical channel name/tagline inside YouTube's central safe area when preparing the final upload-sized banner.

## Thumbnail system
Use four repeatable families rather than unrelated artwork:

1. **Daily Analysis** — XAUUSD TODAY + one strong question/market direction.
2. **News Event** — event acronym (NFP/CPI/FOMC) + direct XAUUSD impact question.
3. **Live Stream** — XAUUSD LIVE + session name.
4. **Education** — one concept such as VCPR + concise explanation promise.

Rules:
- Keep the main text to roughly 3–6 large words where possible.
- XAUUSD/gold must be immediately recognizable.
- Use green/red only to communicate market direction or alerts.
- Gold remains the brand accent.
- Avoid guaranteed-profit wording, exaggerated P/L claims, or misleading win-rate claims.
- Use a consistent bull/logo mark and consistent type hierarchy.
